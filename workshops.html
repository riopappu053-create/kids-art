Workspace Details

Workspace & Studio Guidelines

Everything parents need to prepare before sending their young creator to our weekend intensives.

Are all materials and protective smocks included in the fee?

Yes, 100% of fine Italian rag papers, non-toxic clays, glazes, canvas stretchers, and personalized linen aprons are provided. You never need to supply external art gear.

When can clay sculptures and fired pottery pieces be picked up?

Because ceramics must completely slow-dry for 5 days before undergoing double bisque and glaze firings, items are ready for pickup 10 days after your workshop date.

Can we reschedule a session if our child feels unwell?

Absolutely. With a brief 12-hour notice, workshop credits remain active for 6 months and can be transferred to any future weekend workshop or holiday art camp.
