Our Vetting & Certification Process

Parents trust us with their child's emotional and creative development. Here is how we ensure world-class care in our studios.

Complete Background Checks

Every faculty member completes thorough multi-tier state safety background checks and pediatric child safeguarding screening before entering classrooms.

Fine Arts Degree Mandatory

All teachers hold a minimum of a Bachelor of Fine Arts (B.F.A.) or an early childhood arts education credential from accredited fine arts academies.

Gentle Empathy Training

Instructors undergo biannual workshops on neurodiverse encouragement, patience coaching, sensory sensitivity, and positive speech reinforcement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Parents Ask About Mentors

Everything you need to know about our classroom ratios, instructor assignments, and trials.

Can parents meet the instructor prior to the first session?

Yes! Every complimentary free trial session includes a 15-minute one-on-one parent and instructor chat to discuss your child's favorite mediums, temperament, and personal developmental goals.

What is the student-to-teacher ratio during painting sessions?

We strictly limit early-years workshops to 1 instructor per 5 students, and older youth groups to 1 instructor per 7 students to ensure personalized hands-on brush guidance.

Do instructors assist with missing makeup classes?

Yes. If you inform us 24 hours in advance, students are welcome to attend an open studio makeup session led by our rotating studio mentor team.
