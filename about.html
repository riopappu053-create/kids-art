How We Began

A sanctuary built for fearless creative expression.

Founded by visionary educators and fine artists, LittlePicasso emerged from a fundamental belief: every child is inherently an artist, yet traditional classrooms often reward perfection over curiosity.

We designed our studios as vibrant creative laboratories with open natural lighting, child-height easels, and organic non-toxic mediums where young minds learn to trust their intuition and celebrate individual style.

Our Guiding Pillars

Values at the Heart of Every Class

Our child-centered educational framework fosters creative resilience, spatial awareness, and lifelong artistic appreciation.

Process Over Product

We honor the experimental exploration, brush testing, and color mixing far more than a uniform predetermined outcome.

Empathetic Mentorship

Instructors act as compassionate companions who encourage questions, celebrate self-expression, and listen attentively.

Eco & Toxin-Free

Every wash, pigment, paste, and medium used in studio is hypoallergenic, plant-derived, and strictly eco-conscious.

Courage & Resilience

Learning how to navigate an accidental smudge or blend builds authentic problem-solving and psychological grit.

Our Evolution

A Decade of Colorful Growth

From a single attic art workshop into a distinguished creative academy for young artists.

2016

The Seedling Workshop

Started as a weekend community gathering for 12 local children exploring botanical dyes and mixed pastels.

2019

Flagship Studio Launch

Inaugurated our current daylight studio with dedicated age-group zones and specialized child ergonomics.

2022

Annual Youth Biennial

Partnered with contemporary galleries to stage the first curated exhibition celebrating exclusively juvenile artwork.

Today

1,200+ Artists Strong

Mentoring hundreds of imaginative painters weekly with an unwavering 1:6 educator ratio and personalized tracks.
