Parent Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about class placement, trial procedures, or safety? Here are quick answers.

What if my child has zero prior art experience?

Every single class is beginner-friendly! We design assignments with layered complexity so both beginners and experienced painters progress joyfully at their natural cadence.

Can parents sit inside the studio during classes?

For toddlers, parents may accompany children during the initial trial. For standard sessions, parents enjoy our comfortable adjacent viewing lounge with coffee and one-way glass.

Are all studio paints washable and safe?

Yes, 100%. All paints and pastels used in junior and toddler levels are non-toxic, water-based, and skin-safe, easily washing out of clothes and skin with gentle soap.

What is included in the complimentary trial?

The trial includes a full standard studio session, all canvas mediums, personalized feedback from our educator, and a finished original painting to take home.
