Questions & Answers

Parent Inquiry Help Desk

Find answers to common questions about initial visits, paint safety, class sizes, and attendance policies.

01. What should my child wear to a trial session?

We provide full-coverage waterproof art smocks and aprons. We recommend comfortable casual clothing and closed-toe footwear suitable for creative exploration.

02. Are all art supplies included in course fees?

Yes! All fine-art acrylics, canvases, watercolor papers, glazes, and sculpting clays are 100% included with no hidden equipment or cleanup surcharge.

03. Can parents remain inside the studio?

For Little Explorers (ages 3–5), parents are welcome to sit in the studio. For older cohorts, parents can relax in our viewing lounge with complimentary espresso and Wi-Fi.

04. How do make-up classes work if we miss one?

We offer two flexible make-up tokens per semester with 12 hours advance notice, redeemable during weekend open ateliers or holiday camps.

05. Are the paints and materials non-toxic?

Exclusively ACMI AP-certified non-toxic, hypoallergenic, and water-soluble paints, pastels, and modeling clays are stocked throughout our ateliers.

06. Do you offer private art parties or workshops?

Yes! We host bespoke creative birthday parties and homeschool group workshops on Sundays with customizable art themes and dedicated instructors.
